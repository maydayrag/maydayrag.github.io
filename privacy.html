Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Purpose

This application is used for personal file synchronization between a local computer and Google Drive through rclone.

Google Drive Data

The application may access, read, upload, modify, and delete files in the Google Drive account authorized by the user.

Data Storage

Files are transferred between the user's local computer and Google Drive. OAuth credentials are stored locally by rclone.

Data Sharing

The application is not operated as a public service. The operator does not sell Google Drive user data or use it for advertising.

Data Retention and Deletion

File retention follows the user's synchronization settings. Users can revoke Google account access and delete local OAuth credentials.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the application operator through the contact information provided on the Google OAuth consent screen.
